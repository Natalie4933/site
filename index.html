THE PERSON BEHIND THE SOUND
✧

HELLO, I’M NATALIE

A quiet mind.
A playful spirit.

A love of meditation. A curiosity for sound. A soft spot for the unexpected.

Natalie makes music for the moments when life asks you to slow down. Dreamy drones, gentle rhythms, and the occasional happy accident come together in soundscapes that leave room to breathe—and perhaps a little room to smile.

Meditation is part of her world, and that sense of presence carries into her creative life. Her music explores the space between stillness and movement: a drifting piano, a rounded pulse, a chant that seems to linger in the air.

There’s a playful spark beneath the calm. This is an invitation to listen with curiosity, to let your thoughts wander, and to discover what a small pause can feel like.
